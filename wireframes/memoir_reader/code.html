October 1994

The Paris
Chronicles

A collection of fragmented memories from a brisk autumn along the Seine.

"We walked until our feet ached, letting the city dictate our path."

The air was crisp that morning. We stopped at a small patisserie near the Marais, the smell of butter and burnt sugar clinging to our coats long after we left. I remember you trying to capture the exact shade of the morning light on your old 35mm.

Location

Café de Flore, St-Germain
